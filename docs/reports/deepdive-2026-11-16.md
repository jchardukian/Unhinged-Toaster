## Change made
Dead lead-in trim (owner priority: swipe rate). Before transcription, every clip is analyzed; if it opens still AND
quiet, it now starts ~0.25–0.5 s before the first motion or sound. Limits: at most 3 s, nothing under 0.6 s, always
1 s kept before the detected peak, never under 6 s left, and no trim at all when there is motion or sound from frame 0.
Verified on an encoded test clip (2 s dead opening → 1.28 s trimmed; motion-from-start clip untouched) and by tests.

Expected effect: fewer swipes in the first second on clips that open on a static frame.
